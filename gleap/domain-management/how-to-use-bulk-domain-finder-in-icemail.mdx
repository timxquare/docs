Bulk Domain Finder lets you upload a CSV of domain names to check their availability and purchase multiple domains in a single workflow — ideal for agencies and outbound teams buying at scale.

---

### Step 1: Open Bulk Domain Finder

Navigate to **Domain(s)** → **Buy New Domain(s)** → **Bulk Domain Finder**.

---

### Step 2: Upload Your CSV

- Click the upload area to upload your prepared CSV file, or
- **Download the sample sheet** to see the correct format before uploading your own

The CSV should contain one domain name per row in the correct column format.

---

### Step 3: Scan Availability

Click **Upload All** to start scanning. Icemail will check each domain's availability status automatically.

---

### Step 4: Review Your Cart

Only **available domains** are added to your cart automatically. Unavailable domains are excluded.

Review the cart before proceeding.

---

### Step 5: Complete Payment

Choose your payment method:

- **Wallet**: use your Icemail wallet balance
- **Stripe**: pay securely by card

Confirm to complete the purchase.

---

### Constraints & Notes

- **Maximum 100 domains per CSV** upload
- Only available domains are added to the cart — unavailable ones are excluded automatically
- Incorrectly formatted CSV files will be rejected — use the sample sheet as a reference

---

**Best for:** Agencies and outbound teams purchasing domains at scale.
