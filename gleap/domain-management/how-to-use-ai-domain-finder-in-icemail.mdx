AI Domain Finder uses artificial intelligence to discover available domains suited for cold outreach, based on keywords or an existing primary domain. It saves research time and surfaces brand-aligned options faster.

---

### Access AI Domain Finder

Navigate to **Domain(s)** → **Buy New Domain(s)** → **AI Domain Finder**.

---

### Step 1: Choose Your Input Method

- **By Keywords**: enter brand or product-related terms (e.g. your company name, niche, or campaign focus)
- **By Primary Domain**: enter an existing domain you own + a short brand description for context

---

### Step 2: Configure Preferences

- **TLD preference**: choose from .com, .net, .io, and other available extensions
- **Number of suggestions**: specify how many domain ideas to generate
- **Quantity to purchase**: select how many domains you intend to buy

---

### Step 3: Generate Domains

Click **Generate** and wait **1–2 minutes** for the AI to surface available domain suggestions.

---

### Step 4: Review and Add to Cart

Browse the generated domains, select the ones that fit your brand, and add them to your cart.

---

### Step 5: Proceed to Payment

Click **Proceed to Payment** and choose:

- **Stripe**: secure card payment
- **Wallet**: use Icemail wallet balance

Confirm to complete your purchase.

---

### Benefits

- Saves domain research time significantly
- Surfaces brand-aligned options automatically
- Faster purchasing at scale vs. manual searching
