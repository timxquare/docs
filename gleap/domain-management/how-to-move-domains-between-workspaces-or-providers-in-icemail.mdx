Icemail allows you to move domains across workspaces and switch their provider between Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 — all from within the dashboard.

---

### Critical Requirement

**The domain must not have any active mailboxes.** You must delete all mailboxes associated with the domain before initiating a move.

---

### Steps to Move a Domain

#### Step 1: Go to Domains Tab

Log in to your Icemail dashboard and navigate to the **Domains** tab.

#### Step 2: Select Domain(s)

Use the checkbox to select one or multiple domains you want to move.

#### Step 3: Select 'Move Domains'

From the action menu that appears, click **Move Domains**.

#### Step 4: Choose Target Workspace and Provider

Select your:

- **Target workspace**: the workspace you want to move the domain to
- **Provider**: Google Workspace or Microsoft 365

#### Step 5: Confirm the Move

Review the move details and click **Confirm** to complete the action.

---

### Troubleshooting

If the move is blocked, it is because active mailboxes still exist on the domain.

- Delete all mailboxes on the domain first
- Then retry the move process
