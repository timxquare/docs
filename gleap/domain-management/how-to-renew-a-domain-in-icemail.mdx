Renewing your domain ensures **uninterrupted cold email outreach** and prevents your domain from expiring and becoming unusable.

---

### When Can You Renew?

- **14 days before expiry**: renewal becomes available in your dashboard
- **15-day grace period**: after expiry, you can still renew during this window
- **After grace period**: expired domains are marked unusable and cannot be renewed
- Only domains purchased through Icemail and within 2 months of expiry are eligible for renewal

---

### Step 1: Access the Renewals Section

Log in to Icemail → go to **Domains** → click **Renewals** from the sidebar or tab.

Select the domain(s) you want to renew, then click **Renew Domain**.

---

### Step 2: Review Renewal Details

- **Renewal period**: fixed at 1 year per renewal
- Review the total cost shown on screen
- **Click ****Proceed to Payment** when ready

---

### Step 3: Complete Payment

Choose your payment method:

- **Wallet**: use your Icemail wallet balance
- **Stripe**: pay securely by card

Confirm the payment to complete renewal.

---

### How Renewal Dates Work

Renewal time is **added to your existing expiry date**, not the date of renewal.

Example: if your domain expires **June 1, 2025** and you renew it, the new expiry date will be **June 1, 2026**.
