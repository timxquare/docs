This guide walks you through the exact steps to configure Catch-All email for any domain in **Icemail**.

---

###  **What Is a Catch-All Email?**

A **Catch-All Email** is a special mailbox that automatically receives all messages sent to undefined or non-existent email addresses under a specific domain.

> Example:  
If your domain is `yourdomain.com` and someone emails `ceo@yourdomain.com` (even though no mailbox called "ceo" exists), the Catch-All will receive it.
>
---

###  **Steps to Set Up a Catch-All Email in Icemail**

#### **Step 1: Go to the Domains Dashboard**

Log into your Icemail account and navigate to the **“Domains”** section from the left-hand menu.

---

#### **Step 2: Select Your Domain**

Choose the domain for which you want to enable Catch-All functionality. Click on it to open the settings sidebar.

---

#### **Step 3: Access Domain Settings**

In the sidebar, click on the **“Domain Settings”** tab.

---

#### **Step 4: Add a Catch-All Email Address**

You’ll see a section labeled **Catch-All Email**.

- Enter a valid email address under your connected domain that should receive all unmatched or misaddressed emails.
- Example: `catchall@yourdomain.com` (make sure this mailbox exists in Icemail).
- Click **“Save”** to apply your changes.

 You’ve now successfully enabled Catch-All email for your domain!

---

###  Pro Tips

- **Don’t overuse Catch-All:** While helpful, using Catch-All for high-volume domains can make spam filtering harder. Only use it when necessary.
- **Use filters or labels:** Organize messages received by your Catch-All mailbox to avoid clutter.
- **Test it:** Try sending an email to a fake address under your domain (e.g., `random123@yourdomain.com`) to ensure it's routed properly.
