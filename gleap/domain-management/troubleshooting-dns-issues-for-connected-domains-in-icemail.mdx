When a connected domain shows as 

**Inactive** in your Icemail dashboard, it usually means one of the following:

- NS (Nameserver) records are not pointing to Icemail
- DNS has not yet propagated
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC, or MX records are missing

---

### Step 1: Check Domain Status in Icemail Dashboard

Log in to your Icemail dashboard and navigate to the **Domains** section.

Look for status labels such as:

- **Inactive** — domain not verified yet
- **DNS not propagating** — nameservers updated but not resolved globally

---

### Step 2: Verify Nameservers Match Exactly

Click your domain, then click **Add Nameservers** to see the required nameserver values Icemail provides.

Then update those nameservers at your domain registrar:

- **GoDaddy**: My Products → Domains → DNS → Nameservers → Change → Enter Custom Nameservers
- **Namecheap**: Domain List → Manage → Nameservers → Custom DNS → paste values
- **Cloudflare**: Select domain → Overview → scroll to Nameservers section → update to custom

**Important:** The nameservers must match exactly what Icemail displays — copy-paste to avoid typos.

---

### Step 3: Wait for DNS Propagation

DNS propagation can take **up to 24–48 hours** depending on your registrar and global DNS caches.

To verify propagation status, visit **Whois.com** and look up your domain's current nameserver records.

---

### Step 4: Click Verify in Icemail

Once propagation is confirmed, return to your Icemail dashboard and click **Verify** next to the domain.

---

### ✅ Once Verified

After successful verification:

- Domain status changes to Active
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and MX records are all configured automatically
- Domain is ready to use for cold outreach campaigns
