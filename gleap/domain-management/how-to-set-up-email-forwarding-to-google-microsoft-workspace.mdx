Want all incoming emails from your outreach campaigns delivered to your Gmail or Outlook inbox? **Icemail** makes it easy to forward messages directly to your Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 email account.

This guide walks you through setting up **email forwarding** for any domain inside the Icemail platform.

---

###  What Is Email Forwarding?

**Email Forwarding** automatically redirects incoming emails from one address to another. It's especially useful when:

- You want to centralize all email replies into your primary inbox (e.g., Gmail or Outlook)
- You manage multiple outreach inboxes
- You're using Icemail as a sending platform but prefer replying via your regular workspace

---

###  Steps to Set Up Email Forwarding in Icemail

#### **Step 1: Open the Domains Dashboard**

Log into your Icemail account and click on the **“Domains”** tab in the left-hand menu.

---

#### **Step 2: Select the Domain**

Click on the domain you want to forward emails **from**. A settings **sidebar** will appear.

---

#### **Step 3: Access Domain Settings**

In the sidebar, click on the **“Domain Settings”** tab.

---

#### **Step 4: Set the Forwarding Email**

Under the **Forwarding Email** section:

- Enter the **email address** where you want all incoming messages sent.
> For example: `you@yourcompany.com` (hosted by Gmail or Outlook)
>
- Click **“Save”** to confirm the changes.

That’s it! All emails sent to mailboxes on this domain will now be forwarded to your specified address.

---

###  Best Practices for Email Forwarding

- **Use a monitored inbox**: Make sure the target email (e.g., Gmail/Outlook) is actively monitored to ensure quick responses to warm leads.
- **Enable threading or labels** in Gmail/Outlook to manage forwarded emails easily.
- **Test the setup** by sending a test email to an Icemail-connected mailbox to ensure forwarding is working properly.
