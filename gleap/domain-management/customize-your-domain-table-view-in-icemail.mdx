Managing dozens—or even hundreds—of domains for cold outreach can get overwhelming. That’s why Icemail gives you complete control over your domain dashboard view with our **Column Selection feature**. Focus only on the data that matters to you.

---

###  What’s New?

You can now **choose which columns** appear in your domain table. Whether you're managing SPF, DKIM, DMARC statuses or just want to track mailbox usage, this feature allows you to simplify your dashboard.

**Key Benefits:**

- Tailor your workspace to your workflow
- Reduce visual clutter
- Quickly access only the metrics you care about (e.g., DNS status, warmup status, assigned mailboxes)

---

###  How to Use the Column Selection Feature

#### **Step 1: Go to the Domains Dashboard**

Log into your Icemail account and click on the **“Domains”** tab from the left-hand menu.

---

#### **Step 2: Click on the “Columns” Button**

At the top-right corner of your domain table, you’ll see a button labeled **“Columns.”** Click it to open the customization menu.

---

#### **Step 3: Select or Deselect Columns**

You’ll now see a list of available columns.

- Use the **checkboxes** to turn columns **on or off**
- Choose columns like:
  - **Domain Name**
  - **SPF/DKIM/DMARC Status**
  - **Catch-All Email**
  - **Forwarding Settings**
  - **Warmup Status**
  - **Connected Mailboxes**
  - And more…

---

#### **Step 4: View Updates Instantly**

Once you make your selections, the domain table will **instantly update** to reflect your preferences.

 No need to click “save” — your settings are **automatically saved** and will persist even after logging out or refreshing the page.

---

###  Pro Tips

- Use fewer columns to improve loading speed on large accounts.
- Create views based on team roles—e.g., let your tech ops team view DNS records while SDRs see mailboxes and warmup status.
- Coming soon: Save custom column presets per user/team.
