**Domain masking** is a powerful feature in **Icemail** that lets you use a **secondary domain to represent your primary domain**, hiding the original while maintaining a secure, professional email presence.

This technique helps boost cold email deliverability, protect your main domain from blacklisting, and ensure your links and email headers look credible and clean.

---

###  **What is Domain Masking?**

**Domain masking** makes it appear as though all activity is coming from a different (secondary) domain—even though you're actually using a primary domain behind the scenes. Instead of simply forwarding users or emails, Icemail uses a **proxy layer with SSL encryption** to mask your domain identity.

> For example, instead of exposing `mainbrand.com`, you can send emails from `campaignhost.co`, keeping the main domain private and protected.
>
---

###  **How Domain Masking Works in Icemail**

1. **DNS Setup**  
You point the **A record** of your secondary domain to a trusted Icemail proxy IP.
1. **SSL + Proxy Layer**  
Icemail automatically configures SSL certificates for your secondary domain, establishing a secure connection for email links and headers.
1. **Header & Link Rewriting**  
All links and header references in your emails are rewritten to reflect the masked domain—not your primary one.
1. **IP Management**  
Icemail manages IP pools (shared or dedicated), preserving domain reputation and improving inbox placement.

---

###  **Benefits of Using Domain Masking in Icemail**

####  **Improved Email Deliverability**

- Masked domains help emails pass security filters (e.g., Mimecast, Barracuda).
- Reduces chances of being flagged as spoofed or spam.

####  **Primary Domain Protection**

- Keeps your main domain from being blacklisted.
- Useful when sending at scale across multiple outreach campaigns.

####  **Enhanced Credibility**

- Clean, branded links improve trust and boost reply rates.
- Appears secure thanks to automatic SSL.

####  **Campaign Scalability**

- Easily manage multiple sender domains without revealing your infrastructure.

---

###  **How to Set Up Domain Masking in Icemail**

#### **Step 1: Open the Domains Dashboard**

Log into your Icemail account and navigate to the **“Domains”** section.

---

#### **Step 2: Choose the Domain**

Click on the domain you'd like to use as your **masked domain**. A sidebar will appear.

---

#### **Step 3: Go to Domain Settings**

In the sidebar, click the **“Domain Settings”** tab.

---

#### **Step 4: Enable Domain Masking**

Under the **“Domain Forwarding”** section:

- Enter your **main website URL** (e.g., `https://yourbrand.com`) — this is where users will be redirected if they click a branded link.
- Check the box labeled **“Enable Domain Masking.”**

---

#### **Step 5: Save Your Changes**

Click **“Save”** to apply the settings.  
 Please allow **5–10 minutes** for DNS and SSL propagation. Once completed, domain masking will be active.
