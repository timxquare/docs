In Icemail, using a **custom tracking domain** improves your email deliverability and brand consistency. Tagging mailboxes with **Filter Tags** makes organizing your cold outreach campaigns easier and more scalable.

This guide walks you through how to **verify your custom tracking domain** and **add filter tags** in your Icemail dashboard.

---

###  What Is a Custom Tracking Domain?

A **custom tracking domain** is a branded domain used to track link clicks and open rates in cold email campaigns. Instead of using a generic tracking domain (e.g., `tracking.genericmail.com`), your custom domain could look like:

> `track.yourdomain.com`
>
**Benefits:**

- Boosts credibility and trust with recipients
- Improves inbox placement (reduces spam risks)
- Helps monitor campaign performance at a domain level

---

###  How to View Your Custom Tracking Domain in Icemail

#### **Step 1: Go to the Mailboxes Dashboard**

- Navigate to the **Mailboxes** section from your Icemail dashboard.

#### **Step 2: Hover Over Export Details**

- In the mailbox listing, hover your mouse over the **Export Details** icon next to the domain.
- A **tooltip popup** will appear showing the **Custom Tracking Domain** being used.

That’s it! You’ve confirmed the active tracking domain for your email campaigns.

---

###  How to Add a Filter Tag to a Mailbox

Filter tags let you **organize mailboxes by campaigns, clients, teams, or strategies**, helping you track performance at scale.

#### **Step 1: Go to the Mailboxes Dashboard**

- From the left-hand menu, open the **Mailboxes** section.

#### **Step 2: Select the Email to Tag**

- Choose the email account you want to organize using a filter tag.

#### **Step 3: Click on “Filter Tags: All”**

- This opens the tag management interface.

#### **Step 4: Click on “Create New Tag”**

- Enter your **tag name** (e.g., “LeadGen – June” or “Client A – SDR 1”).
- Choose a **color** to visually organize your tags.
- Click **Create**.

#### **Step 5: Apply the Tag to a Domain**

- Select the domain(s) or mailboxes to which you want to assign the tag.
- Click **Add Tag**, then select the tag you created.
- Click **Add** again to confirm.

#### **Step 6: Verify**

- You’ll now see the assigned tag next to the associated mailbox or domain.

---

###  Why Use Filter Tags?

- **Organize at Scale**: Group mailboxes by campaign, region, SDR, or account.
- **Faster Filtering**: Quickly filter your dashboard views by tag.
- **Better Insights**: Measure domain-level performance with clarity.
