Purchasing a new domain with Icemail is quick and effortless—designed to have you set up and ready to send cold emails in moments. Just follow these steps:

### 1. Navigate to the Domains Page

Log into your Icemail account and head to the **Domain Management** section from the main dashboard.

### 2. Open the Domain Purchase Tool

Click on **“Add Domain”** (top-right corner), then choose **“Buy New Domain”** from the dropdown menu ([icemail.ai](https://www.icemail.ai/Privacy-Policy?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [icemail.ai](https://www.icemail.ai/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [help.icemail.ai](https://help.zapmail.ai/en/articles/9445944-guide-to-connect-existing-domains?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [help.icemail.ai](https://help.zapmail.ai/en/articles/9527479-how-to-buy-a-new-domain-in-zapmail-a-step-by-step-guide?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

### 3. Search for a Domain

Type your desired domain name (e.g. `yourcompany-email.com`) into the search bar. Icemail will present a list of available options, listing variations and TLDs to suit your preferences .

### 4. Pick Your Favorite

Review the suggested domains, select the one that aligns with your brand or campaign (e.g., `coldreach-yourcompany.com`), and click **“Add to Cart”** .

### 5. Review Your Cart

Click **“View Cart”** to double‑check:

- Chosen domain name
- Registration period (1 year, 2 years, etc.)
- Total cost

Once everything looks good, click **“Proceed to Buy”** ([youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FsuZX8y6dyk&utm_source=chatgpt.com), [help.icemail.ai](https://help.zapmail.ai/en/articles/9527479-how-to-buy-a-new-domain-in-zapmail-a-step-by-step-guide?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

### 6. Choose a Payment Method

You can pay via:

- **Wallet Credit** (if you’ve previously added funds)
- **Stripe** (secure card processing)

Choose your preferred method and proceed ([icemail.ai](https://icemail.ai/Terms-%26-Conditions?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [help.icemail.ai](https://help.zapmail.ai/en/articles/9527479-how-to-buy-a-new-domain-in-zapmail-a-step-by-step-guide?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

### 7. Confirm Payment

Enter your card or Stripe details, then click **“Pay.”** Icemail will process your transaction immediately ([icemail.ai](https://www.icemail.ai/Privacy-Policy?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

###  That’s It! Your Domain is Live

You’ll receive a confirmation once the domain is successfully registered, and it will appear under your domain list—ready to be connected to any mailbox or cold email campaign using [**team@icemail.ai**](mailto:team@icemail.ai).
