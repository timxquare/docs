This step-by-step guide will help you configure **domain forwarding** within **Icemail**, allowing you to seamlessly redirect visitors from one of your domains to another—ideal for branding, redirecting cold traffic, or consolidating engagement.

---

###  **What is Domain Forwarding?**

**Domain forwarding** allows you to automatically redirect traffic from one domain (e.g., `yourcampaign.io`) to another main domain (e.g., `yourcompany.com`). This is especially helpful when:

- You want to preserve your sender reputation while redirecting engagement.
- You’re using multiple outreach domains and want to funnel users to a central destination.
- You’re running A/B tests on branded domains and need one endpoint.

---

###  **How to Set Up Domain Forwarding in Icemail**

#### **Step 1: Go to the Domains Dashboard**

Log in to your **Icemail** account and click on the **“Domains”** section in the left-hand navigation.

#### **Step 2: Select the Domain to Forward From**

Find the domain you want to redirect. Click on it to open the domain details.

#### **Step 3: Open Domain Settings**

Click **“See More”** to expand the domain sidebar.  
Then, go to the **“Domain Settings”** tab.

#### **Step 4: Enter Your Target (Main) Domain**

In the **Domain Forwarding** section, enter the full URL of the main domain you want to redirect visitors to (e.g., `https://yourcompany.com`).

Make sure to include the full protocol (`http://` or `https://`).

#### **Step 5: Save Your Settings**

Click **“Save.”** Your domain forwarding setup is now active.

 All traffic to your chosen domain will now redirect to the main domain you specified.

---

###  Notes and Best Practices

- DNS propagation is not required for forwarding—changes are effective almost immediately.
- Forwarding works only for **web traffic** (not email). To forward emails, see our [Email Forwarding Guide](#).
- For branding purposes, consider using **domain masking** (see related article below).
