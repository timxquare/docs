Managing dozens or even hundreds of cold outreach domains? Icemail makes it fast and scalable with our **bulk domain connection feature**. You can upload multiple domains at once using a CSV file and let Icemail handle the rest — including automated SPF, DKIM, and DMARC setup.

This guide walks you through each step.

---

###  **Step 1: Log into Your Icemail Dashboard**

- Navigate to the **“Domains”** tab on the left-hand menu.
- Click on the **“Connect Domains”** button at the top right.

---

###  **Step 2: Bulk Upload Domains**

- Click **“Bulk Upload Domains”** to begin.
- Download the **Sample CSV file** provided to see the required format.
- Fill in your domain list following the sample structure (e.g., just the domain names in a single column).
- Upload the completed CSV file to proceed.

Once uploaded, Icemail will start importing and listing the domains automatically.

---

###  **Step 3: Update Nameservers at Your Registrar**

After your domains are added to Icemail, you’ll need to **replace the NS (Nameserver) records** with the ones provided in your dashboard.

>  This step enables **automatic configuration** of your domain's SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records for optimal email deliverability.
>
####  Name Server Update Guides:

- [How to Change NS Records in GoDaddy](#)
- [How to Change NS Records in Hostinger](#)
- [How to Change NS Records in Namecheap](#)

 DNS propagation can take **up to 24–48 hours**.

---

###  **Step 4: Recheck All Domains**

After updating the NS records in your domain registrar:

- Go back to Icemail.
- Click the **“Recheck All”** button in the Domains section.
- Icemail will verify the updates and confirm domain connection status.

---

###  **Important Note for Cloudflare Users**

If your domain was purchased **directly through Cloudflare**, **domain masking is not possible** because Cloudflare doesn’t support external nameserver changes.

To connect the domain to Icemail:

- **Transfer** your domain to a registrar that allows NS updates (e.g., GoDaddy, Namecheap).
- Then update the name servers and reconnect in Icemail.

---

###  Common Errors & How to Fix Them

####  **1. Workspace Already Exists**

- This means the domain is active under another Icemail workspace.
- Remove or delete the associated mailboxes before reconnecting.

####  **2. NS Records Not Updated**

- Double-check that you've updated the NS records correctly.
- Use [whatsmydns.net](https://www.whatsmydns.net/) to confirm global propagation.

####  **3. Domain Already Connected**

- This domain is already connected to your (or another) Icemail account.
- Check your domain list or contact support to resolve duplicates.

---

###  **Next Step: Add Mailboxes**

Once your domains are successfully connected, you can proceed to:

- Navigate to the **Mailboxes** tab
- Click **“Add Mailboxes”** > **Buy New Mailboxes** based on your outreach needs
