## This guide will walk you through how to connect your existing domains to **Icemail**, giving you centralized control over your email outreach infrastructure and ensuring maximum deliverability for your cold email campaigns.

---

###  **Why Connect an Existing Domain to Icemail?**

Connecting your domain to **Icemail** enables:

-  **Seamless email outreach** using your branded or previously purchased domain
-  **One-click mailbox creation** and warmup automation
-  **Automatic configuration** of all essential DNS records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX)
-  **Centralized management** of all domain and mailbox activity within the Icemail dashboard

---

###  **Step-by-Step: Connecting Your Domain to Icemail**

#### 1. **Access the Domain Management Panel**

Log into your Icemail dashboard and go to the **“Domains”** section.

#### 2. **Click “Add Domain” → “Connect Domain”**

This will launch the process to link an external domain you already own.

#### 3. **Enter Your Domain**

Input your existing domain name (e.g., `yourcompanydomain.com`) and click **“Connect Domain.”**

#### 4. **Update Your Nameservers (NS Records)**

You’ll now need to **replace your domain’s current NS (Name Server) records** with the ones provided by Icemail.

> **Important:** Updating NS records allows Icemail to automatically set up and manage your DNS records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and MX) for optimal email deliverability.
>
#### 5. **How to Change NS Records in Your Domain Registrar**

Here are links to detailed guides depending on where you purchased your domain:

- [GoDaddy: Change NS Records](#)
- [Namecheap: Change NS Records](#)
- [Hostinger: Change NS Records](#)

 **Note:** DNS propagation typically takes **24–48 hours**. Your domain may not connect immediately.

#### 6. **Cloudflare Users – Read Carefully**

If your domain was registered **through Cloudflare**, you **cannot connect it directly** to Icemail, as Cloudflare does not allow changing NS records for domains they manage.

To use your domain with Icemail:

1. Transfer your domain to a registrar that supports NS changes (e.g., GoDaddy or Namecheap).
1. Once transferred, proceed with the connection process in Icemail.

#### 7. **Verify Domain Connection**

After updating the NS records, return to the Icemail dashboard and click **“Recheck.”** Our system will automatically verify that the correct NS records have been applied.

#### 8. **Click “Continue” to Finalize**

Once verification is successful, click **“Continue”** to complete the setup. Your domain is now connected to **Icemail** and ready for mailbox creation.

###  **Common Errors & Troubleshooting**

Here are some common messages you might encounter and what they mean:

---

####  **Workspace Already Exists**

This means your domain is currently active in another Icemail workspace or is associated with existing mailboxes elsewhere.  
**Solution:**

- Contact your registrar and **delete any active mailboxes**.
- Ensure the domain is not in use in another platform or Icemail workspace.
- Reach out to [**team@icemail.ai**](mailto:team@icemail.ai) for help in clearing workspace conflicts.

---

####  **NS Records Not Updated**

The system can’t detect the correct NS records.  
**Solution:**

- Double-check that you've updated the **exact** NS records provided in the Icemail dashboard.
- Use [https://whatsmydns.net](https://whatsmydns.net) to verify global propagation.
- Allow up to **48 hours** for changes to fully propagate.

---

####  **Domain Already Connected**

Your domain may already be linked to an existing Icemail account.  
**Solution:**

- Check your Icemail domain list to avoid duplication.
- If needed, email [**team@icemail.ai**](mailto:team@icemail.ai) to help investigate ownership.

###  **Next Step: Add Mailboxes**

1. Navigate to the **Mailboxes** section.
