Welcome to Icemail’s frequently asked questions on **account setup and configuration**. Whether you're just getting started or fine-tuning your cold email infrastructure, here are answers to the most common setup-related queries.

---

###  Do I get access to the Google Admin console?

**Yes.** When you onboard mailboxes through Icemail, you receive **full access to the Google Admin Console** for your domain. This allows you to manage users, security settings, groups, and more.

---

###  Can I set a profile picture for my mailboxes using Icemail?

Absolutely. Icemail lets you **add or update profile pictures** for each mailbox. This helps improve trust and personalization in your cold outreach efforts.

---

###  How many mailboxes per domain are recommended?

We recommend **3 mailboxes per domain** to ensure optimal deliverability and sending limits. However, depending on your strategy, **1–5 mailboxes per domain** is also considered safe and effective.

---

###  Can I set up custom tracking domains?

Yes, custom tracking domains are fully supported. Icemail works seamlessly with platforms like:

- **ReachInbox**
- **Instantly**
- **Smartlead**

For other platforms, contact our support team at [team@icemail.ai](mailto:team@icemail.ai) — we’ll help you configure your tracking domain correctly.

---

###  How can I set up forwarding domains in Icemail?

It’s quick and easy:

1. Go to the **Domains** tab in your dashboard.
1. Select the domain you want to configure.
1. Click **Edit**, then add the URL where you’d like incoming traffic or emails to be redirected.

This is useful for domain reputation management, fallback routing, or centralizing replies.
