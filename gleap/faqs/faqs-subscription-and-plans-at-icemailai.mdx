Whether you're just starting or scaling your cold email operations, understanding how Icemail’s subscription plans work is essential. This article answers common questions about mailbox limits, billing cycles, upgrades, and cancellations.

---

###  How many mailboxes and domains do I need?

Not sure where to start? Use the **[Icemail Mailbox Calculator](https://icemail.ai/cold-email-mailbox-calculator)** — enter your monthly contact reach and sequence length, and the calculator will tell you exactly how many mailboxes and domains you need, along with estimated costs across all mailbox types (Google, Microsoft, Azure, SMTP).

---

###  Is there a limit to the number of mailboxes I can set up with Icemail?

No hard limits! Icemail is built to scale with your outreach needs. Google and Microsoft mailboxes are priced at **$2.50/mailbox/month**, and you can **stack or upgrade** plans to add as many mailboxes as needed.

You only pay for the capacity you use, with no cap on how many plans you can purchase.

---

###  I want to upgrade my plan. How does that work?

Upgrading your plan is seamless and flexible.

#### Here’s an example:

If you’re on a **$39 plan** (10 mailboxes/month) that started on the **2nd of the month** and later upgrade to a **$99 plan** (30 mailboxes/month) on the **10th**, you'll get **30 additional mailboxes immediately**.

Each plan will maintain its original billing cycle:

- The $39 plan renews on the 2nd of every month.
- The $99 plan renews on the 10th of every month.

 _Pro Tip:_ You can stack multiple plans based on your mailbox requirements and cancel or upgrade them individually.

---

###  Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes, absolutely. Icemail gives you full control over your subscription.

- You can **cancel any plan at any time**.
- Your services will remain active until the **end of the current billing cycle**.
- No penalties, no hidden fees.

 Just head to your **Billing Dashboard**, choose the plan, and click **Cancel Subscription**.
