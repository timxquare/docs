AI Auto-Filling creates realistic mailbox identities automatically, saving time when setting up multiple mailboxes for cold outreach.

---

### Access AI Auto-Filling

Navigate to **Mailboxes** → **Assigned** → **Assign Mailboxes** → **Auto-Fill Using AI** tab.

Select your platform first: **Google Workspace** or **Microsoft 365**.

---

### Option 1 – Name-Based Generation

- Enter the number of mailboxes to create
- Provide first and last names to use as a basis
- Select the domain(s) to assign mailboxes to
- Optionally enable AI profile picture generation, or upload custom pictures

The AI will generate email usernames, display names, and professional formatting automatically.

---

### Option 2 – Gender & Ethnicity-Based Generation

- Select gender preference (Male, Female, or Both)
- Choose ethnicity for more natural, human-like name generation
- Specify the quantity of mailboxes

The AI generates first names, last names, and email usernames based on your selections.

When both genders are selected, you can adjust the **male-to-female ratio** to match your outreach persona mix.

Ideal for cold outreach agencies and growth teams managing multiple personas at scale.

---

### After Generation

- Review generated names and usernames
- Select the assignment domain
- Make manual edits if needed before confirming
- Click **"Assign Mailboxes"** to place the order

---

### Important Warning

**Once an order is placed, it cannot be reverted or deleted.** Please review all details carefully before confirming.

---

### Additional Notes

- Works for both single and bulk mailbox creation
- Takes approximately 1–2 minutes to provision
