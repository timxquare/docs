Logging into your **Google Workspace mailboxes** directly is essential when testing deliverability, verifying setup, or integrating with third-party tools. This guide will walk you through how to securely retrieve your credentials and complete a manual login—using **Icemail + Google Authenticator**.

---

###  Step-by-Step Login Guide

#### **Step 1: Select Mailboxes in Icemail**

- Go to your **Mailboxes Dashboard**.
- Select one or more Google Workspace mailboxes you want to access manually.

---

#### **Step 2: Export Mailbox Credentials**

- Click the **“Mailbox Actions”** button.
- Choose **“Export Mailboxes”**, then click **“Export CSV File”**.
- You’ll receive a secure CSV file sent to your registered Icemail admin email.

---

#### **Step 3: Check Your Email & Download the CSV**

- Open the CSV file. It includes:
  - **Email addresses**
  - **Passwords**
  - **Google Authenticator codes** for 2FA login

>  Keep this file secure—it contains sensitive login credentials.
>
---

#### **Step 4: Log Into Gmail.com**

- Visit [https://mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com).
- Enter the email and password provided in the CSV.

---

#### **Step 5: Use Google Authenticator Code from Icemail**

- When prompted for **2FA**, go back to your Icemail dashboard.
- Click **“See More”** on the right side of the selected mailbox.
- You’ll see the **current 6-digit Google Authenticator code**.

> Paste this code into the Google prompt to complete login.
>
---

###  Tips for Smooth Login

- **Authenticator code expires every 30 seconds**—if it fails, refresh and try again.
- Make sure your **browser is not auto-filling old credentials**.
- If login fails, reset the password via Icemail, export the CSV again, and retry.

---

###  Use Cases for Manual Login

- Confirm mailbox delivery and inbox placement
- Check spam folder behavior
- Manually configure third-party tools like Lemlist, Instantly, Woodpecker, etc.
- Access Gmail settings (e.g., signature, vacation responder, forwarding rules)
