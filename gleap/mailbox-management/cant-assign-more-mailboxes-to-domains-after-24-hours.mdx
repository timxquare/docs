[Skip to main conte](https://help.zapmail.ai/en/articles/9687824-can-t-assign-more-mailboxes-to-domains-after-24-hours#main-content)When using our service, you may encounter a situation where you are unable to assign more mailboxes to domains after 24 hours. This is due to our monthly based billing system, which does not allow us to add mailboxes to a workspace in the middle of a billing cycle.

 

Each domain has a workspace associated with it, and our billing system is designed to bill for the entire workspace at once. This means that if you add a new mailbox to a domain in the middle of a billing cycle, it would create a discrepancy in our billing system.

 

As a result, we have implemented a restriction that prevents the addition of new mailboxes to a workspace after 24 hours have passed since the start of the billing cycle. This ensures that our billing system remains accurate and consistent for all of our customers.

 

If you need to add more mailboxes to a domain, we recommend doing so before the start of the next billing cycle. This will ensure that the new mailboxes are included in the billing for that cycle and will not cause any discrepancies.

  
Here are a some other reasons why this limitation might be in place:

 

## 1. Stopping Spam

Limiting the number of mailboxes you can create in a day helps stop spam. Some people try to make lots of mailboxes really fast to send out tons of unwanted emails. With a limit, we can help keep out spammers!

 

## 2. Keeping the System Running Well

Creating too many mailboxes at once can slow things down for everyone. By setting a 24-hour limit, we make sure the system stays steady and works well for everyone.

 

## 3. Preventing Fraud

This limit also helps keep things fair. It stops people from making lots of mailboxes to get extra free trials or special offers.

 

## 4. Ensuring Quality

By controlling how many mailboxes are created in a day, we can better check that all mailboxes are working as they should, without overloading the system.

With this limit, everyone can enjoy a smoother, safer experience!

 

**Note: This limitation is specifically imposed by Google and may not apply to all service providers.**
