Managing your cold outreach infrastructure often includes retiring unused or outdated mailboxes. This guide walks you through how to **safely delete mailboxes in Icemail**, whether you're cleaning up your workspace or removing mailboxes no longer in use.

---

###  Before You Begin

- **Deleted mailboxes cannot be recovered** once the process completes.
- **Admin mailboxes** trigger deletion of the entire associated workspace.
- Ensure there are **no unresolved mailbox errors** before initiating deletion.

---

###  Steps to Delete a Mailbox in Icemail

#### **Step 1: Open the Mailboxes Dashboard**

- Log in to your **Icemail** account.
- Navigate to the **“Mailboxes”** section from the left-hand menu.

---

#### **Step 2: Select the Mailbox You Want to Delete**

- Click on the mailbox you intend to remove.
- This will open a detailed sidebar for that mailbox.

---

#### **Step 3: Click “Delete Mailbox”**

- In the sidebar, click the **“Delete Mailbox”** button near the bottom.

---

#### **Step 4: Confirm the Deletion**

- A confirmation dialog will appear showing the mailbox details.
- Click **“Delete”** to proceed.

---

#### **Step 5: Monitor Deletion Status**

- The mailbox status will change to **“Deletion in Progress.”**
- You can track the deletion under the **Status column** in your dashboard.

---

###  Deletion Timelines & Conditions

- **Scheduled Deletion:** Deletions are queued and typically completed within **24 hours**.
- **Mailboxes with Errors:** If the mailbox has unresolved errors, deletion will fail. Please resolve issues first.
- **Admin Mailboxes Warning:** Deleting a mailbox marked as **Admin** will also **delete the entire workspace** attached to it.
