This guide walks you through how to **schedule multiple mailboxes at once** using a CSV file in Icemail. It’s the perfect solution for teams planning in advance or managing high-volume mailbox creation across campaigns.

---

###  Why Use This Feature?

- Queue up multiple mailboxes for creation on your **next billing cycle**.
- Save time with **bulk uploads**.
- Align mailbox provisioning with your **email marketing or operational timelines**.

---

###  Step-by-Step: Schedule Multiple Mailboxes via CSV

#### **Step 1:**

From the **dashboard**, navigate to the **Mailboxes** section in the left sidebar.

#### **Step 2:**

Click on **“Schedule Mailboxes”**.

#### **Step 3:**

Click **“Upload Bulk CSV”** to open the upload tool.

#### **Step 4:**

Upload your CSV file:

- You can **drag and drop** the file or click to **browse your device**.
- Click **“Download Sample CSV”** if you need a reference format.  
Make sure each row includes:
  - First Name
  - Last Name
  - Username
  - Domain

#### **Step 5:**

Review your entries in the preview window. If everything looks good, click **“Upload All.”**

#### **Step 6:**

Click **“Assign Mailboxes”** to queue them for automatic creation on your **next billing date**.

---

###  How to View Scheduled Mailboxes

You can view all scheduled mailboxes by clicking on **“View Scheduled Mailboxes”** from the same section.

---

###  Important Notes

- Mailboxes will be **created after your next billing date**, not immediately.
- You may **edit or remove** scheduled mailboxes any time before provisioning.
- If your **subscription is inactive** or **no seats are available**, mailbox creation will be skipped.
- Make sure your CSV file matches the **required format** to avoid upload errors.

---

###  You're All Set!

Your mailboxes are successfully scheduled and will be provisioned automatically on your next billing cycle. For changes or to review the queue, head over to the **Scheduled Mailboxes** section.
