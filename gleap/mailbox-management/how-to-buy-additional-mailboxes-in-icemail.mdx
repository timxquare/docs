Need more mailboxes for your campaigns or team members? Icemail makes it easy to purchase and assign additional mailboxes to your domains—whether you're scaling outreach or onboarding a new team.

---

###  Steps to Buy Additional Mailboxes

#### **Step 1: Go to the Mailboxes Dashboard**

Log in to your **Icemail** account and click on the **“Mailboxes”** tab from the left-hand menu.

---

#### **Step 2: Click “Buy New Mailboxes”**

At the top of the Mailboxes dashboard, click the **“Buy New Mailboxes”** button.

---

#### **Step 3: Choose Your Subscription Plan**

You’ll be redirected to the purchase screen. Here's what to do:

- **Select a Plan:** Choose between **Monthly** or **Yearly** billing.
- **Enter Quantity:** Type in how many mailboxes you want to buy.
- **Select Mailbox Type:** Choose from:
  - **Google Workspace** (Licensed Google Business Starter – Official Google Partner) — **$2.50/mailbox/month**, US IP
  - **Microsoft 365** (Microsoft 365 Business Starter plan) — **$2.50/mailbox/month**, US IP
  - **SMTP** (Dedicated IP per domain, up to 10 mailboxes/domain) — Contact us for pricing
  - **Azure** (100 mailboxes per domain, $30/domain/month)
- **Review Pricing:** All Google and Microsoft mailboxes are priced at **$2.50/mailbox/month**
- **Click “Pay Now”** to complete your purchase.

---

 Once payment is confirmed, the new mailboxes will appear in your dashboard and can be assigned to any connected domain.

---

###  Pro Tips

- **Plan Wisely:** If you're scaling fast, consider yearly billing for cost savings.
- **Assign Mailboxes Immediately:** Head to the domain section to assign your newly purchased mailboxes.
- **Need Bulk Mailboxes?** Contact support for volume pricing or enterprise options.
