The **Scheduled Mailbox** feature allows you to **queue the creation of mailboxes** for your **next billing cycle**. This is ideal when you're close to account limits or want to plan mailbox provisioning ahead of time.

###  What is the Scheduled Mailbox Feature?

Instead of waiting until your next cycle to add mailboxes manually, you can schedule them in advance. Icemail will **automatically create them** when your new billing period begins.

---

###  When Should You Use It?

- You’ve hit the **24-hour limit** for mailbox assignments.
- You want to **pre-plan** your infrastructure for an upcoming campaign.
- You need **auto-provisioning** aligned with your subscription billing cycle.

---

###  How to Schedule a Mailbox

#### **Step 1:**

Go to the **Mailboxes** section from your Icemail dashboard.

#### **Step 2:**

Click on **“Schedule Mailbox”** in the left sidebar or the main Mailbox Actions menu.

#### **Step 3:**

Fill in the details:

- **First Name**
- **Last Name**
- **Username**
- **Assign Domain(s)**

Then click **“Assign Mailbox”** to confirm the schedule.

---

###  How to View Scheduled Mailboxes

Click on **“View Scheduled Mailboxes”** from the same Mailbox section to see all pending mailbox assignments.

---

###  Important Notes

- Mailboxes will be created **after your next billing date**, not immediately.
- You can **edit or remove** scheduled mailboxes anytime before they're provisioned.
- Ensure your **plan is active and seats are available**—scheduled mailboxes won’t be created if your subscription lapses or limits are exceeded.
