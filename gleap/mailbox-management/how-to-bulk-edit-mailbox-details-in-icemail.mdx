## Managing multiple mailboxes just got easier. With Icemail’s **Bulk Edit** feature, you can quickly update names, usernames, and profile pictures across several mailboxes—perfect for onboarding teams, refreshing account identities, or standardizing mailbox formats.

## 

---

###  What Can You Update?

Using bulk editing, you can modify the following details for each selected mailbox:

- **First Name**
- **Last Name**
- **Username**
- **Profile Picture**

---

###  Steps to Edit Mailboxes in Bulk

#### **Step 1: Go to Mailboxes Dashboard**

Log in to your **Icemail** account and head to the **Mailboxes** section.

---

#### **Step 2: Select the Mailboxes**

- Use the checkboxes to select all the mailboxes you want to edit.
- You can filter or sort by domain, status, or other metadata for easier selection.

---

#### **Step 3: Click "Mailbox Actions" → "Edit Mailboxes"**

- After selecting mailboxes, click the **“Mailbox Actions”** button at the top.
- From the dropdown, choose **“Edit Mailboxes.”**

---

#### **Step 4: Update Mailbox Information**

- A bulk editing interface will appear.
- Update the desired fields for each mailbox:
  - First Name
  - Last Name
  - Username
  - Upload or choose a profile picture

>  Tip: You can copy/paste values between fields or use patterns (e.g., `first.last@domain.com`) for consistency.
>
---

#### **Step 5: Click “Save” to Apply Changes**

- After reviewing your updates, click **“Save.”**
- All selected mailboxes will be updated immediately.

 Done! Your mailboxes are now refreshed with new details.

###  Best Practices

- **Use Naming Conventions:** Keep formatting consistent across usernames for easier search and team management.
- **Profile Pictures:** Add recognizable images to humanize your outreach and boost deliverability.
- **Audit Regularly:** Schedule bulk edits periodically to keep data clean and updated.
