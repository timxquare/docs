If you’re managing large-scale cold outreach, it’s important to keep your mailbox infrastructure clean. This guide explains how to **delete multiple mailboxes at once in Icemail**, helping you stay organized and maintain optimal performance across your workspace.

---

###  Before You Start

> **Important Notes:**
>
- **Deletion is permanent.** Once processed, deleted mailboxes **cannot be recovered.**
- **Deletion may take up to 24 hours** to complete.
- **Mailboxes with errors** must be fixed before they can be deleted.
- **Admin mailboxes:** Deleting an admin mailbox will also delete the **entire workspace** it's tied to.

---

###  Steps to Bulk Delete Mailboxes in Icemail

#### **Step 1: Go to the Mailboxes Dashboard**

- Log in to your [Icemail account](https://icemail.ai).
- Navigate to the **“Mailboxes”** section from the left-side menu.

---

#### **Step 2: Select the Mailboxes You Want to Delete**

- Use the checkboxes next to each mailbox to select multiple mailboxes.
- You can also use the “Select All” checkbox at the top of the table for mass selection.

---

#### **Step 3: Click on “Mailbox Actions” → “Delete Mailboxes”**

- After selecting the mailboxes, click the **“Mailbox Actions”** button.
- Choose **“Delete Mailboxes”** from the dropdown.

---

#### **Step 4: Review and Confirm**

- A pop-up window will appear listing all selected mailboxes.
- Review the list carefully to make sure you’re not deleting critical or admin mailboxes.
- Click **“Delete”** to confirm and schedule them for deletion.

---

###  What Happens Next?

- Mailboxes will enter a **“Scheduled for Deletion”** status.
- Deletion typically completes within **24 hours**.
- You can monitor progress in the **Status** column in your Mailboxes Dashboard.

---

###  Pro Tips

- **Resolve all mailbox errors** before bulk deletion to avoid issues.
- **Double-check for admin roles** before proceeding.
- Use filters or labels to sort and select mailboxes more efficiently.
