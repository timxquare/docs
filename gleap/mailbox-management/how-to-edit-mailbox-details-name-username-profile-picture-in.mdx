## Keeping your mailbox identity consistent and professional is essential—especially when sending cold emails at scale. This guide shows you how to **update the first name, last name, username, and profile picture** for your mailboxes within **Icemail**.

---

###  Why Update Mailbox Details?

Personalizing your mailbox improves:

- **Email deliverability and trust**
- **Consistency across outreach campaigns**
- **Professional appearance to recipients**

---

##  Editing an Individual Mailbox in Icemail

Follow these steps to edit your mailbox details:

---

### **Step 1: Navigate to the Mailboxes Dashboard**

- Log in to your Icemail account.
- Click on the **“Mailboxes”** tab in the left-hand menu.

---

### **Step 2: Select the Mailbox**

- Click on the **specific mailbox** you'd like to update.
- A details sidebar will slide in from the right.

---

### **Step 3: Click on “Edit Details”**

- In the sidebar, locate and click the **“Edit Details”** button.

---

### **Step 4: Update Mailbox Information**

You’ll now be able to edit the following fields:

- **First Name** (e.g., _John_)
- **Last Name** (e.g., _Doe_)
- **Username** (e.g., [_john.doe@yourdomain.com_](mailto:john.doe@yourdomain.com))
- **Profile Picture** (upload a custom avatar or logo)

---

### **Step 5: Click “Update Details”**

- Once all changes are made, click **“Update Details”** to save them.

 That’s it! Your mailbox is now updated with the latest display information.

---

###  Pro Tips

- Use a **realistic sender name** to build trust (e.g., _Sarah from Acme Corp_).
- A **professional headshot or logo** as the profile picture can improve open rates.
- Keep usernames consistent with your domain strategy (e.g., `firstname.lastname@yourdomain.com`).
