For enhanced security and smoother third-party integrations, **Icemail now supports secure SMTP and App Password access** for all your mailboxes. With built-in **2FA (Two-Factor Authentication)**, your credentials are now safer and easier to manage.

This guide walks you through how to export a CSV containing your **login credentials, app passwords**, and **SMTP details**.

---

###  Prerequisites

- Make sure 2FA is enabled on your Icemail account (it is enabled by default).
- Only Admin users can export credentials.
- The mailboxes you want to export must be **fully active** and free of setup errors.

---

###  Steps to Export SMTP & App Passwords

#### **Step 1: Select Your Mailboxes**

- Navigate to your **Mailboxes Dashboard**.
- Use the checkboxes to select one or multiple mailboxes for which you want to download credentials.

---

#### **Step 2: Click “Export Mailboxes”**

- Once selected, click the **“Mailbox Actions”** button.
- From the dropdown, choose **“Export Mailboxes.”**

---

#### **Step 3: Export CSV File**

- A prompt will appear. Click on **“Export CSV File.”**
- The export request will be queued, and a download link will be sent to your registered email.

---

#### **Step 4: Check Your Email**

- Within a few minutes, you’ll receive an email with a CSV file attached.
- The file will contain **three tabs (sheets)**:

---

###  CSV File Contents

 

**Sheet** **Details**

**1. Mailbox Credentials**

Regular email login credentials (email + password)

**2. App Passwords**

Secure App Passwords used for tools that don’t support 2FA (like IMAP clients)

**3. SMTP Credentials**

SMTP server, port, username, and password for each mailbox

---

###  Use Cases for These Credentials

- Connect to email sending platforms like **Reply.io, Woodpecker, Lemlist, Instantly, and Smartlead**.
- Set up **custom SMTP** in outreach or CRM tools.
- Use app-specific passwords with **IMAP/SMTP email clients** or **mobile apps**.

---

###  Security Best Practices

- **Do not share CSVs** via unsecured channels.
- Store the file securely (preferably encrypted).
- Revoke and regenerate passwords if credentials are leaked or outdated.
- Avoid reusing SMTP credentials across unrelated services.
