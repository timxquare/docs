Manually logging into your **Microsoft 365 mailbox** is essential when troubleshooting deliverability, accessing inbox settings, or integrating your account with third-party platforms. This guide walks you through the process using your **Icemail dashboard** and **Microsoft's secure login system**.

---

###  Prerequisites

 You have an **Icemail account**  
 You have access to the email where the exported credentials will be sent  
 You're using a Microsoft 365 mailbox

---

###  Step-by-Step Login Process

#### **Step 1: Go to the Icemail Mailboxes Dashboard**

- Log in to your **Icemail** account.
- Navigate to the **Mailboxes Dashboard**.
- Switch to the **Microsoft Workspace** tab.

---

#### **Step 2: Export Mailbox Credentials**

- Select the mailboxes you want to access.
- Click **“Mailbox Actions” → “Export Mailboxes”**.
- Click **“Export CSV File”**, then confirm with **“Continue”**.

>  A secure CSV file will be sent to your admin email address containing:
>
> - Email addresses
> - Passwords
> - Microsoft Authenticator codes (2FA)
>
---

#### **Step 3: Open the CSV File**

- Download and open the CSV file.
- Locate the email and password for the account you want to log in to.

---

#### **Step 4: Go to Microsoft Admin Login**

- Visit [https://admin.microsoft.com](https://admin.microsoft.com)
- Enter the email and password from the CSV.

---

#### **Step 5: Use Microsoft Authenticator Code from Icemail**

- Microsoft will now prompt: **“Enter the code from your authenticator app.”**
- Return to Icemail’s Mailboxes Dashboard.
- Find your mailbox, click **“See More”** on the right side.
- Copy the **6-digit Authenticator code** (specific to that mailbox).

>  **Important:** The code refreshes every 30 seconds. Make sure to enter it promptly.
>
---

####  Success!

Once you enter the correct authenticator code, your mailbox will be successfully logged in. You can now:

- Access inbox messages
- Set up forwarding or filters
- Confirm deliverability
- Use the mailbox in third-party outreach tools

---

###  Tips & Troubleshooting

- **Code expired?** Refresh the authenticator code by clicking “See More” again.
- **Invalid password?** Re-export the CSV and confirm the latest credentials.
- **Using multiple mailboxes?** Repeat the steps per mailbox—each has a unique authenticator code.
