Setting up cold outreach mailboxes is a core part of managing your domains in Icemail. Whether you're adding a few mailboxes or uploading them in bulk for scale, this guide will walk you through both single and bulk creation workflows.

---

###  Before You Begin

Make sure:

- You have already connected your domain(s) to Icemail.
- You know whether you’re assigning **Google Workspace**, **Microsoft 365**, or **Icemail-hosted** mailboxes.

---

##  Creating a **Single Mailbox** in Icemail

Follow these steps to create and assign a single mailbox:

---

### **Step 1: Navigate to the Mailbox Dashboard**

- Log in to your Icemail account
- From the left-hand menu, click on **“Mailboxes.”**

---

### **Step 2: Click on “Assign Mailbox”**

- This button allows you to manually create and link a mailbox to a domain.

---

### **Step 3: Fill Out the Mailbox Details**

- Enter the following:
  - **Email address** (e.g., `jane@yourdomain.com`)
  - **Domain** to associate the mailbox with
  - **Password** (if you’re creating mailboxes directly through Icemail)
  - **Optional Tags** for grouping (e.g., SDR1, Campaign A)

>  **Note:** Ensure all fields are accurate. Typos can cause login issues and domain conflicts.
>
---

### **Step 4: Click “Assign Mailbox”**

- The mailbox will be created and linked to your selected domain.

---

##  Creating **Multiple Mailboxes (Bulk Upload)**

For scaling your campaigns, bulk mailbox creation is the most efficient option.

---

### **Step 1: Click on “Upload Bulk CSV”**

- In the Mailboxes section, you’ll find the **Bulk Upload** option. Click it to begin.

---

### **Step 2: Download the Sample CSV**

- Click “Download Sample CSV” to understand the exact structure required.
- The sample file includes fields like:
  - `Email`
  - `Domain`
  - `Password`
  - `Tag` (optional)

---

### **Step 3: Prepare Your CSV File**

- Fill in your mailbox data following the format exactly.
- Double-check for typos and make sure all domains listed are already connected in Icemail.

---

### **Step 4: Upload the CSV & Click “Assign Mailboxes”**

- Once your file is ready, upload it and hit **“Assign Mailboxes.”**
- Icemail will automatically generate and assign all listed mailboxes to their respective domains.

 Mailboxes will now appear in your dashboard, ready to be used for warmup or cold outreach.

---

###  Pro Tips

- **Use tags** to organize mailboxes by campaign, SDR, or region.
- **Enable auto warmup** after mailbox creation to improve deliverability.
- If you're using third-party services (like Google or Microsoft), complete manual login after mailbox setup for proper verification.
