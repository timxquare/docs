Icemail SMTP Mailboxes give you dedicated IP infrastructure per domain, designed for teams who need reliable, high-volume cold email sending with full control over their sending environment.

---

### 🔑 Key Features

- **Dedicated IP** per domain for maximum deliverability control
- **Up to 10 mailboxes** per domain
- **Daily Sending Limit:** Up to 50 emails/day per mailbox
- **Warmup:** 15 warmup emails/day per mailbox (1:1 ratio with cold outreach)
- Automated DNS, DKIM, DMARC, and SPF configuration

---

### 📧 Recommended Sending Limits

Follow the 1:1 warmup-to-outreach ratio for best deliverability:

| Activity | Emails/Day/Mailbox | | --- | --- | | Cold Outreach | 15 | | Warmup | 15 | | **Total** | **50 max** |

**Parallel warmup is always recommended.** Run warmup and cold outreach simultaneously in equal portions.

---

### ⚙️ Domain & Mailbox Configuration

- Each domain gets a **dedicated IP address**
- Configure up to **10 mailboxes per domain**
- DNS records (DKIM, DMARC, SPF) are set up automatically

---

### 💼 Who Is This For?

SMTP Mailboxes are ideal for:

- **Experienced senders** who want dedicated IP control
- **Teams** that need higher per-mailbox sending volume (up to 50 emails/day)
- **Agencies** requiring isolated sending infrastructure per domain
