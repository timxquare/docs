Whether you need to cancel your subscription, change your mind and revert a cancellation, or clean up your dashboard by deleting old subscriptions—this guide will walk you through the entire process.

---

###  How to Cancel Your Subscription

If you decide to cancel your Icemail subscription, follow these steps:

####  Step-by-Step

1. **Go to the Billing Section**  
Navigate to the **Billing** tab in the left-hand sidebar of your Icemail dashboard.
1. **Click “See More”**  
Under the **Active Subscription** you'd like to cancel, click **See More** to view plan details.
1. **Select “Cancel Subscription”**  
Scroll to the bottom and choose the **Cancel Subscription** option.
1. **Confirm Cancellation**  
A confirmation prompt will appear. Click **Confirm** to complete the process.

> **Note:** Your subscription will stay active until the end of the current billing cycle. No additional charges will occur after that.
>
---

###  How to Revert a Cancellation

Changed your mind? You can **reactivate** a scheduled-to-cancel subscription anytime before it officially ends.

####  Steps to Revert

1. **Return to Billing**  
Open the **Billing** tab from your dashboard.
1. **Find the Subscription Marked “Scheduled to Cancel”**  
Look for the subscription with that status under your billing history.
1. **Click “Revert Cancellation”**  
This will immediately stop the cancellation process, and your plan will continue uninterrupted.

> **Note:** You won’t lose any data, mailboxes, or settings during this process.
>
---

###  How to Delete a Cancelled Subscription from Your Dashboard

Want to tidy up your dashboard? You can permanently remove cancelled plans you no longer need.

####  How to Delete

- Click the ** Bin Icon** next to the cancelled subscription.
- Confirm the deletion when prompted.

>  **Warning:** This action is irreversible. Deleting a cancelled subscription will erase **all historical billing and plan data** associated with it.
>
---

###  You're All Set!

Your subscription updates have been saved. Cancelled plans you delete will no longer appear in your account view. If you need further assistance, our support team is here for you at [team@icemail.ai](mailto:team@icemail.ai).
