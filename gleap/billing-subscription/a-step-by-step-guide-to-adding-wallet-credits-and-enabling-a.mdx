Icemail offers a **wallet credit system** to help you manage mailbox renewals with ease. By adding funds in advance and enabling **Auto-Topup**, you’ll never have to worry about service interruptions or failed payments again.

---

###  How to Add Wallet Credits

1. **Go to the Wallet Section**
  - Log in to your Icemail dashboard.
  - From the left-hand menu, navigate to **Billing > Wallet**.
1. **Add Funds to Your Wallet**
  - Click on the **“Add More Balance”** button.
  - Enter the amount you'd like to deposit (e.g., $50, $100, etc.).
  - Click **“Add Balance”**.
1. **Complete the Payment**
  - You’ll be redirected to the secure payment page.
  - Enter your card details and confirm the transaction.
  - Once complete, your wallet will be instantly credited, and you’ll see the updated balance in your dashboard.

---

###  How to Set Up Auto-Topup

1. **Access Auto-Topup Settings**
  - Go to **Settings > Wallet**.
  - Locate and enable the **Auto-Topup** toggle.
1. **Edit Auto-Topup Preferences**
  - Click on **“Edit Auto-Topup”** to configure your preferences.
1. **Configure Your Settings**:
  - **Minimum Wallet Balance:**  
Set the threshold amount. If your wallet balance falls below this, an auto-topup will be triggered.  
_Example: $20_
  - **Auto-Recharge Amount:**  
Specify how much should be added when the threshold is reached.  
_Example: $50_
1. **Save Your Settings**
  - Click **“Update Auto-Topup”** to activate.

---

###  Why Use Wallet Credits?

-  **No Missed Renewals** – Your mailboxes renew automatically without manual payment.
-  **Secure Transactions** – One-time setup; no need to re-enter card details every time.
-  **Effortless Budgeting** – Prepay for mailbox usage and control your cash flow.
