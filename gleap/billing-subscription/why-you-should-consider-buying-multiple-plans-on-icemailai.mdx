At **Icemail**, we understand that businesses grow at different paces and have diverse cold outreach needs. That’s why we support the flexibility of stacking multiple plans under a single account.

Whether you're scaling your outbound efforts or managing multiple teams or clients, this guide will walk you through the **benefits and important details** of buying multiple plans.

---

###  Benefits of Purchasing Multiple Plans

#### 1. **Flexibility Across Teams or Use Cases**

Each Icemail plan can be tailored to a specific use case—whether it’s for SDRs, marketing experiments, A/B testing, or managing multiple client accounts.

 **Example:** One plan can be configured for lead generation, another for client onboarding, and another for cold follow-ups.

---

#### 2. **Cost Optimization**

Instead of committing to a larger, all-inclusive plan (which may include features you don’t need), you can choose **smaller, targeted plans** that fit your exact needs—often saving you money.

 Pay only for what you use, avoiding overprovisioned, underutilized subscriptions.

---

#### 3. **Scalable Growth Without Downtime**

As your business expands, your cold outreach infrastructure should scale smoothly. Stacking plans allows you to grow gradually without reconfiguring everything from scratch.

 Add new plans as new sales reps or teams come onboard.

---

#### 4. **Access to Specialized Features**

Some plans include unique features or mailbox limits suited for specific goals. By stacking, you gain access to **different feature sets** under the same account umbrella.

 Example: A Premium plan for automation, and a Starter plan for manual control.

---

###  Important Notes About Stacking Icemail Plans

####  All Plans Run Concurrently

When you purchase multiple plans, **each plan is active at the same time**, providing access to the mailboxes, sending limits, and tools included in each.

---

####  Plans Are Billed Separately

Each plan comes with its own pricing and billing cycle. Be sure to review the **total cost** if you’re managing several subscriptions.

---

####  24-Hour Upgrade Window

If you want to **upgrade a plan** (for example, to increase mailbox limits), you must do so **within 24 hours** of the initial purchase. After that, you'll need to purchase a new plan instead of upgrading an existing one.

---

###  Pro Tip

If you're managing multiple client projects or teams, using stacked plans allows for **separation of resources**, reducing the risk of domain conflicts, IP warming delays, or deliverability issues—all managed from a single dashboard.
