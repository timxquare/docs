Icemail makes upgrading your plan simple and flexible, offering two convenient options: **Instant Upgrade** and **Scheduled Upgrade**. Whether you're scaling rapidly or planning ahead, you can adjust your plan to suit your growing business needs.

---

###  Upgrade Options

####  Instant Upgrade

Upgrade your plan immediately and unlock new features, mailbox limits, or performance benefits without waiting for the next billing cycle.

- **How it works:**  
You'll be charged **only the difference** between your current base plan and the new plan (prorated). The new plan is applied **immediately**, giving you instant access to the additional features.
- **Use Case:**  
Perfect for users who want to **scale quickly**, onboard more mailboxes, or unlock premium features right away.

---

####  Scheduled Upgrade

Schedule your upgrade to activate on your **next billing cycle**—ideal for budgeting ahead or avoiding immediate charges.

- **How it works:**  
You won’t be charged now. The new plan will take effect **on your upcoming billing date**, at which point you’ll be charged the **full price** of the upgraded plan.
- **Use Case:**  
Ideal for users who want to **upgrade later** without interrupting current billing or workflow processes.

---

###  How to Upgrade Your Plan

#### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Navigate to Billing**  
From your Icemail dashboard, go to the **Billing** section in the left sidebar.
1. **Select Active Subscription**  
Click **"See More"** on the subscription you'd like to upgrade.
1. **Click “Upgrade Plan”**  
You’ll now see the available upgrade options.
1. **Choose Upgrade Method**  
Select either:
  - **Instant Upgrade** (charged now), or
  - **Schedule Upgrade** (charged on the next billing date).

---

###  If You Choose **Instant Upgrade**

1. A window will display the **prorated amount** based on the price difference between your current and target plans.
1. Click **“Upgrade Now”** to confirm.
1. Select your **payment method**:
  - **Credit/Debit Card via Stripe**
  - **Icemail Wallet** (if wallet balance is available)
1. Click **Continue** to finalize.

 Your plan will be upgraded immediately after successful payment.

---

###  If You Choose **Schedule Upgrade**

1. A confirmation window will appear. Click **“Schedule Upgrade”**.
1. Your new plan will be **queued to go live** on your next billing date.
1. In the **Billing** section, you’ll see the **scheduled upgrade** status and the exact **activation date**.
1. You may cancel this upgrade **anytime** before the scheduled date if you change your mind.
