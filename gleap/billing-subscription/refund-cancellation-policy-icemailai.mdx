At **Icemail.ai**, we’re committed to delivering an exceptional experience in mailbox provisioning and cold email automation. However, we understand that plans can change. Below, you’ll find important information about our **No Refund** and **Cancellation** policies.

---

###  No Refund Policy

All payments made to **Icemail.ai** are **final**. This includes:

- Monthly or annual subscription charges
- Mailbox setup or add-on service fees
- Prepaid credits or wallet top-ups

We **do not offer refunds**, either full or partial, once a transaction is completed. We strongly encourage users to review their selections and plan terms before completing a purchase.

> Note: This policy applies whether or not the service was used or accessed after payment.

---

###  Mailbox Refund Policy – Once a Mailbox is Used

Once a mailbox has been **provisioned and used** — meaning it has been accessed, configured, connected to a sending tool, or had any emails sent from it — it is **no longer eligible for a refund**.

**What counts as "used":**
- Logging into the mailbox
- Connecting the mailbox to a third-party platform (Instantly, Smartlead, Lemlist, etc.)
- Sending any emails (warmup or cold outreach) from the mailbox
- Configuring custom settings, signatures, or SMTP credentials

**Genuine case exceptions:** Refund requests may be considered on a case-by-case basis at Icemail’s sole discretion for situations such as:
- Duplicate charges due to billing system errors
- Mailboxes that were never provisioned after payment
- Charges made in error that can be verified by our team

To request a review of a genuine billing error, contact [team@icemail.ai](mailto:team@icemail.ai) with your account details and a description of the issue.

---

###  Deliverability Refunds – Not Accepted

Refund requests based on **poor mailbox deliverability** or **email performance** will **not be accepted**.

Here’s why: Once a mailbox is configured and handed over to you, its deliverability is entirely within your control. Factors that affect deliverability — such as email content, sending volume, warmup practices, list quality, and recipient engagement — are all determined by how you use the mailbox.

**Icemail is not responsible for deliverability outcomes resulting from:**
- Sending cold emails before completing the warmup period
- Exceeding recommended sending limits (15 emails/day cold + 15 emails/day warmup for Google & Microsoft)
- Using purchased or low-quality email lists
- Sending spam-like or non-compliant email content
- Assigning more than 2–3 mailboxes per domain (which can increase domain-level risk)
- Ignoring bounce rates or spam complaint signals

We provide all the technical infrastructure — properly configured SPF, DKIM, DMARC, US IP addresses, and Google/Microsoft-licensed accounts — to give your mailboxes the best possible starting point. How you use them from there determines the results.

> **Best Practice:** Always complete a full warmup period and follow [Cold Email Best Practices](https://help.icemail.ai) before launching campaigns at scale.

---

###  Cancellation Policy

If you decide to discontinue your service with Icemail:

- You can cancel your subscription at any time from your **Billing Dashboard**.
- Cancellation will **stop all future billing** but will **not refund** any amount already paid.
- Your service access will continue until the end of your current billing cycle.

For example:  
If you cancel on June 10th and your plan renews on June 30th, you’ll retain access until June 30th with **no further charges** after that date.
