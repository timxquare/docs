Managing your billing details is simple and secure with **Icemail**. Whether you need to update your payment method, change your billing address, or download past invoices, this guide will walk you through everything step-by-step.

---

###  How to Update Your Card Details

1. **Go to Billing Settings**
  - From your Icemail dashboard, navigate to the **Billing** tab in the left-hand sidebar.
1. **Access the Billing Portal**
  - Click on **“View Invoice”** or **“Manage Billing”** to open the secure Icemail billing portal.
1. **Edit or Add Card Information**
  - In the billing portal, click the ** Edit** icon next to your current card details.
  - To add a new card, click **“Add Payment Method”**, enter your card information, and click **“Update”**.
  - Your payment method will now be updated for future billing cycles.

---

###  How to Update Your Billing Information

1. **Go to the Billing & Shipping Info Section**
  - In the billing portal, scroll down to the section titled **“Billing & Shipping Information.”**
1. **Edit Billing Details**
  - Click on **“Update Information.”**
  - Enter the updated billing address, company name, tax details (if applicable), and any other relevant information.
1. **Save Changes**
  - Once updated, click **“Save”** or **“Update”** to apply the new billing information instantly.

---

###  How to Check Your Invoice History

1. **Scroll to the Invoices Section**
  - At the bottom of the billing portal, locate the **“Invoice History”** section.
1. **View or Download Past Invoices**
  - You’ll see a list of all past invoices along with their dates, amounts, and statuses.
  - Click **“View”** or **“Download PDF”** next to any invoice for your records or accounting needs.

---

###  That’s It!

You’ve successfully managed your billing and payment details in **Icemail**. Keeping this information up to date ensures uninterrupted access to your account and services.
