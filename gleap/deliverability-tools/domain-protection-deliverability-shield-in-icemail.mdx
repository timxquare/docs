Icemail's Deliverability Shield protects your outreach domains by continuously monitoring risk factors that affect inbox placement and sender reputation.

---

### What Deliverability Shield Protects

- **Blacklist Monitoring**: get notified immediately if your domain appears on major blocklists
- **DNS Security Monitoring**: detects DNS misconfigurations, potential hijacking risks, or suspicious record changes early
- **Deliverability Protection**: maintains strong domain reputation for improved inbox placement over time

---

### Eligible Domains

- Domains purchased through Icemail
- Domains connected via direct integrations: Namecheap, Name.com, Dynadot, Spaceship, Cloudflare

---

### Accessing Deliverability Shield

Navigate to: **Icemail dashboard** → **Deliverability** → **Deliverability Shield** (left sidebar).

---

### Activating Protection

- Select your domain and click **Activate Deliverability Shield**
- Review the pricing details, protection features, and prorated billing information
- Click **Add Shield** to confirm
- Complete payment via Stripe (card) or Wallet

Protection activates immediately after payment is confirmed.

---

### Managing Your Subscription

View your protected domains, subscription status, and renewal dates from the **Billing** section of your dashboard.

---

### Canceling Protection

To cancel Deliverability Shield:

- **Billing** → **Deliverability Shield Tab**
- Click the three-dot menu next to the domain → **Cancel Subscription**
- Check the terms checkbox → type **"Cancel"** in the confirmation field → confirm

**Note:** Protection stops immediately upon cancellation. No further charges will be applied.
