Welcome to Icemail! This guide walks you through the complete setup process — from signing up to sending your first cold email.

---

### Step 1: Sign Up

- Visit **icemail.ai** and click **Get Started**
- Choose a plan that matches your needs and complete checkout
- Access your dashboard via the link sent to your email

---

### Step 2: Complete Workspace Onboarding

- Name your workspace (e.g. **"Outreach Team"** or **"Sales Mailboxes"**)
- Tell us how you found Icemail
- Select your role: Founder, Agency Owner, Marketer, Sales Lead, etc.
- Click **"Continue Setup"** to proceed

---

### Step 3: Set Up Domains

You have two options for adding domains:

#### Option A – Buy New Domains

- Click **Buy Domains** → **Next**
- Browse available domains across TLDs (.com, .net, .org, .info, and more)
- Select your preferred domains and add them to cart
- Complete payment via Wallet or Stripe

Best practices:

- **2–3 mailboxes per domain** is recommended for healthy sending
- **.com domains** are preferred for maximum deliverability
- Purchased domains appear in your dashboard automatically

#### Option B – Connect Existing Domains

- Click **Connect Domains** → **Next**
- Enter your domain name and click **Connect Domain**
- Copy the Icemail nameservers shown in your dashboard
- Update nameservers at your registrar (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Google Domains, etc.)
- DNS propagation can take up to 24 hours
- Click **Recheck** after propagation to verify status

Common issues:

- **Workspace Already Exists**: remove existing mailboxes from the domain or contact support
- **NS Records Not Updated**: wait 24–48 hours and verify using a DNS checker tool
- **Domain Already Connected**: contact support at team@icemail.ai

---

### Step 4: Create Mailboxes

Once your domains are active:

- Click **Create New Mailbox**
- Enter first name, last name, and username
- Select the domain to assign the mailbox to
- Click **Add New** to configure additional mailboxes in bulk
- Click **Assign Mailboxes** to confirm

The system automatically configures SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and MX records for every mailbox.

---

### Step 5: Export Mailboxes to Outreach Tools

- Select the mailboxes you want to export
- Click **Export Mailboxes**
- Choose your platform: ReachInbox, Instantly, Smartlead, and more
- Authenticate with the platform
- Mailboxes export automatically — no manual configuration needed

---

**You're all set!** Your mailboxes are ready to send cold emails. For questions or assistance, contact us at **team@icemail.ai**.
