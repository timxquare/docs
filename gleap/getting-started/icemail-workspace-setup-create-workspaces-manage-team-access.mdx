A workspace is your central hub for domains, mailboxes, subscriptions, and team members. You can create multiple workspaces for different clients, teams, or projects.

---

### Creating a Workspace

- Open the workspace selector dropdown and click **Create Workspace**
- Enter a workspace name (e.g. **"Outbound Team"**, **"Client A"**, **"Sales Ops"**) and proceed
- Select or add contact details
- Review and finalize

Each workspace has its own domains, mailboxes, users, and subscriptions — completely isolated from other workspaces.

---

### Managing Your Workspace

Go to **Settings** → **Workspace** to manage your workspace settings.

From the three-dot menu, you can:

- **Edit Workspace**: update the workspace name or contact details
- **Switch Workspace**: move between your workspaces
- **Delete Workspace**: only available when all mailboxes are removed, all domains are disconnected, and the subscription is canceled

---

### Managing Team Members

**Only Workspace Owners** can invite and manage team members.

#### Adding Team Members

- Click the user icon with plus sign (top of the workspace panel)
- Select the workspace(s) to grant access to
- Enter the email address(es) of the users to invite
- Assign a role:

- **Viewer**: view-only access to the workspace
- **Editor**: can manage mailboxes and domains
- **Admin**: full access including billing and settings

You can invite multiple users and grant access to multiple workspaces in a single step.

#### After Invitation

- New users receive an email invite and complete their account setup
- Existing Icemail users can log in immediately after the invite is accepted

#### Modifying or Removing Access

Go to **Settings** → **Workspace** → **Manage Users** to update a team member's role or revoke their access. Changes take effect immediately.

---

### Leaving a Workspace

To leave a workspace you're a member of: **Settings** → **Workspace** → three-dot menu → **Leave Workspace** → confirm.
