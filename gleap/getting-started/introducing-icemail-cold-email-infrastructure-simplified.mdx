### What is Icemail?

Icemail is an all-in-one cold email infrastructure platform. It provides ready-to-send Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 mailboxes — plus SMTP and Azure mailboxes — with all deliverability-critical configurations handled automatically.

- **Google Workspace**: Licensed Google Business Starter, Official Google Partner
- **Microsoft 365**: Business Starter accounts, fully provisioned
- **SMTP Mailboxes**: dedicated IPs for full control
- **Azure Mailboxes**: for high-volume sending
- **US IP Mailboxes**: available at $2.50/mailbox/month for Google and Microsoft

---

### What You Can Do with Icemail

- Establish mailboxes rapidly — from domain purchase to ready-to-send in minutes
- Auto-configure SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and MX records for every domain
- Connect existing domains or purchase new ones directly
- Add profile pictures to mailboxes for authenticity
- Export mailboxes to leading platforms: ReachInbox, Instantly, Smartlead, Reply.io, and more

- **Seamless Export**: one-click export to leading cold email platforms
- **Scalable**: manages infrastructure for one mailbox or hundreds
- **Transparent Pricing**: $2.50/mailbox/month for Google & Microsoft; Azure at $30/domain/month
