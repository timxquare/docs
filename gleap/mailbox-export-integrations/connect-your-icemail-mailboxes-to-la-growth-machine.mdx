Connecting your Icemail mailboxes to **La Growth Machine** lets you run powerful cold email campaigns with your fully provisioned, US-IP mailboxes—without any manual credential entry.

---

### Step-by-Step: Connect Icemail Mailboxes to La Growth Machine

#### **Step 1:**

Go to your **Mailbox Dashboard** in Icemail and select the mailboxes you want to connect to La Growth Machine.

#### **Step 2:**

Click **"Export Mailboxes"** to view your credentials.

#### **Step 3:**

Log in to your **La Growth Machine** account (lagrowthmachine.com) and go to **Settings → Identities** or **Email Accounts**.

#### **Step 4:**

Click **"Add Email Account"** or **"Connect Email Identity."**

#### **Step 5:**

Select your connection method:

**For Google Workspace mailboxes**: Click **"Connect with Google"** and sign in with your Icemail Google Workspace email address and password. Grant La Growth Machine the required permissions.

**For Microsoft 365 mailboxes**: Click **"Connect with Microsoft"** and authenticate with your Icemail Microsoft 365 email and password.

**For SMTP mailboxes**: Select **"Custom SMTP"** and enter the SMTP/IMAP credentials provided by Icemail.

#### **Step 6:**

La Growth Machine will verify the connection. Once confirmed, the mailbox is ready for use in multichannel sequences.

#### **Step 7:**

Configure your **daily email volume** within La Growth Machine to match Icemail's recommendation of **15 cold emails per mailbox per day.**
