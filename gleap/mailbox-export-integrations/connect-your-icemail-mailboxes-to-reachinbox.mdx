Exporting mailboxes from Icemail to **ReachInbox** helps you streamline outreach, unify your campaign tools, and optimize deliverability. Here’s how to do it in just a few steps.

---

###  Step-by-Step: Export Icemail Mailboxes to ReachInbox

#### **Step 1:**

Go to the **Mailbox Dashboard** and **select** the mailboxes you want to export by checking the boxes next to them.

#### **Step 2:**

Click on the **“Export Mailboxes”** button at the top of the interface.

#### **Step 3:**

From the platform options, **choose “ReachInbox.”**

#### **Step 4:**

Click **“Export”**, and you’ll be prompted to **log in to your ReachInbox account**.

#### **Step 5:**

Once logged in, you’ll see a confirmation message that your mailboxes are now queued for export. They’ll automatically appear in your ReachInbox dashboard once processing is complete.
