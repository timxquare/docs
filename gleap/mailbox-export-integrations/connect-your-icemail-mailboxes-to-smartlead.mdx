Exporting your mailboxes to **Smartlead** enables powerful email automation and multichannel campaign capabilities. Follow these quick steps to integrate seamlessly.

---

###  Step-by-Step Guide: Export to Smartlead

#### **Step 1:**

Go to your **Mailbox Dashboard** in Icemail.  
Select the mailboxes you want to export by checking the boxes beside each one.

#### **Step 2:**

Click on **“Export Mailboxes”** at the top right.

#### **Step 3:**

From the list of integrations, choose **“Smartlead.”**

#### **Step 4:**

Click **“Export”** and log in to your **Smartlead** account when prompted.

#### **Step 5:**

After a successful login, a confirmation message will appear indicating your mailboxes will be exported shortly.

>  _The export process may take a few moments depending on the number of mailboxes selected._
>
