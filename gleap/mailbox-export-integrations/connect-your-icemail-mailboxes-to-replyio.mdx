Integrating your mailboxes with **Reply.io** makes it easy to launch and manage outbound campaigns without manual setup. Follow these streamlined steps to export your Icemail mailboxes directly to Reply.io.

---

###  Step-by-Step Guide

#### **Step 1:**

Navigate to your **Mailbox Dashboard** in Icemail.

#### **Step 2:**

Select the mailboxes you want to export by checking their corresponding boxes.

#### **Step 3:**

Click on the **“Export Mailboxes”** button.

#### **Step 4:**

From the platform list, select **“Reply.io.”**

#### **Step 5:**

Click **“Export”** and log into your **Reply.io** account when prompted.

#### **Step 6:**

Once the export begins, you'll see a confirmation and can monitor progress in the **Export History** section.

---

###  Why Connect to Reply.io?

-  Automate email outreach with minimal setup
-  Transfer credentials securely in seconds
-  Track mailbox integration status in real-time
-  Sync your workflow across Icemail and Reply.io

---

###  Notes

- Ensure your Reply.io account is active and has space to accept new mailboxes.
- Exports are processed instantly, but sync time may vary depending on volume.
- Only Admins or users with Export permission can initiate exports.
