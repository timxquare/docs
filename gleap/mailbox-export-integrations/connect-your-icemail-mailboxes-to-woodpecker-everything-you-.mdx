Integrating your cold email mailboxes from **Icemail** with **Woodpecker** is quick and effortless. Whether you're migrating for outreach campaigns or syncing your mailbox activity with Woodpecker’s automation features, this guide will walk you through every step.

---

###  Why Connect Icemail to Woodpecker?

Connecting your Icemail-managed mailboxes to Woodpecker enables:

- Automated cold email outreach.
- Centralized tracking and reply monitoring.
- Smooth mailbox authentication using OAuth (secure and password-free).
- One-click setup with zero technical overhead.

---

###  Step-by-Step Guide to Export Icemail Mailboxes to Woodpecker

#### 1. **Select Mailboxes**

From your Icemail dashboard, navigate to the **Mailboxes** section and select the mailboxes you'd like to export. You can select multiple mailboxes if needed.

#### 2. **Initiate Export**

Click the **“Export”** button at the top of the interface. This opens the third-party integration panel.

#### 3. **Choose Woodpecker as Destination**

From the list of supported platforms, click on **Woodpecker**.

#### 4. **Authenticate with Woodpecker**

You will be prompted to enter your **Woodpecker login credentials**. Once authenticated, Icemail will automatically begin the export process.

> If you have multiple workspaces in Woodpecker, make sure to select the correct one during this step.
>
#### 5. **Export Confirmation**

After submitting your credentials, you will receive a confirmation message indicating that the mailbox export has started. You can monitor the progress under **Export History** in your Icemail dashboard.

---

###  Notes

- **OAuth-based Integration**: Icemail uses OAuth to securely connect your Google or Microsoft mailboxes, eliminating the need for passwords or 2FA during exports.
- **Real-time Syncing**: All mailbox configurations, including sending limits, warm-up schedules, and alias details, are preserved wherever applicable.
- **Retry Logic**: If an export fails due to platform-side issues, Icemail will automatically retry the export up to three times.
