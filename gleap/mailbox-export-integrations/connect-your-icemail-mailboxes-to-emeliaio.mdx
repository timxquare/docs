Seamlessly export your Icemail mailboxes to **Emelia.io** to manage cold outreach campaigns without manual setup. Follow the steps below to ensure a smooth mailbox integration.

---

###  Step-by-Step Guide to Export Mailboxes

#### **Step 1: Select Mailboxes**

From your **Mailbox Dashboard**, select the checkboxes next to the mailboxes you wish to export.

#### **Step 2: Click “Export Mailboxes”**

Locate and click the **Export** button at the top of the mailbox list.

#### **Step 3: Choose Platform**

From the export options, choose **Emelia** as the destination platform.

#### **Step 4: Authenticate Your Emelia Account**

You’ll be prompted to log in to **Emelia.io**. Enter your credentials and authorize the connection.

#### **Step 5: Export & Monitor Progress**

Click **“Export”** again. Your mailboxes will be queued for transfer.  
Check the **Export History** tab to view export progress and confirmation.

---

###  Why Export to Emelia.io?

-  Save time syncing your email infrastructure
-  Simplify outreach workflows with a connected setup
-  Secure export with credential verification
-  Track export status easily via your Icemail dashboard

---

###  Things to Keep in Mind

- Ensure you have an active **Emelia.io** account with enough capacity to receive new mailboxes.
- Only users with export permissions can perform this operation.
- Exports may take a few minutes depending on volume and network speed.
