Effortlessly integrate your Icemail mailboxes with **Warmy.io**, an email warm-up and deliverability platform. This guide walks you through a secure and seamless mailbox export process.

---

###  Steps to Export Mailboxes to Warmy.io

#### **Step 1: Select Your Mailboxes**

From the **Mailbox Dashboard**, check the boxes next to the mailboxes you want to export.

#### **Step 2: Click on “Export Mailboxes”**

Use the top action bar to initiate the export process.

#### **Step 3: Choose “Warmy.io” as Platform**

From the list of available platforms, select **Warmy.io** as your export destination and click **Export**.

#### **Step 4: Authenticate Your Account**

You'll be prompted to enter your **Warmy.io account credentials**. Add them and click **Update** to start the export.

#### **Step 5: Monitor Export Status**

Navigate to the **Export History** section to see the status of your mailbox transfers. Once complete, your mailboxes will be active in Warmy.io.

---

###  Things to Remember

- You must have a valid Warmy.io account and active plan.
- Only users with the appropriate export permissions can access this feature.
- Mailbox syncing may take a few minutes depending on size and network conditions.

---

###  Conclusion

Exporting mailboxes from **Icemail to Warmy.io** is fast, secure, and user-friendly. Follow the steps above to keep your deliverability operations running efficiently.
