Exporting your mailboxes to **Instantly** enables seamless email outreach, powerful campaign control, and centralized email management—all with just a few clicks.

---

###  Step-by-Step: Export Mailboxes to Instantly

#### **Step 1:**

Head to the **Mailbox Dashboard** inside Icemail and **select** the mailboxes you wish to export by checking the boxes next to them.

#### **Step 2:**

Click on **“Export Mailboxes”** at the top.

#### **Step 3:**

From the platform list, select **“Instantly.”**

#### **Step 4:**

Click **“Export”** and log in using your **Instantly account credentials.**

#### **Step 5:**

If your Instantly account has multiple organizations, you’ll be asked to **choose the destination organization**.

>  _Note: If you only have one organization, mailboxes will be exported there by default._
>
#### **Step 6:**

Once confirmed, you’ll see a **success message**. Your mailboxes will now be exported to the selected Instantly organization. They’ll appear shortly once processing is complete.
