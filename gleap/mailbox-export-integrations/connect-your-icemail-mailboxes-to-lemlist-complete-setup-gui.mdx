Seamlessly export your **Icemail** mailboxes to **Lemlist** for streamlined cold email campaigns. This guide breaks down each step, ensuring a smooth integration and quick setup.

---

###  Why Integrate with Lemlist?

- **Centralized Outreach Management**: Send campaigns from Lemlist using mailboxes managed by Icemail.
- **Secure Credential Export**: Avoid manual SMTP/IMAP setup and reduce configuration errors.
- **Scalable Workflow**: Sync multiple mailboxes at once with one-click export.

---

###  Step-by-Step Guide

#### **1. Select the Mailboxes in Icemail**

- Go to the **Mailboxes** section in your Icemail dashboard.
- Tick the boxes next to the mailboxes you wish to export.

#### **2. Start the Export Process**

- Click the **“Export Mailboxes”** button at the top of the table.

#### **3. Choose Lemlist**

- From the list of supported platforms, select **Lemlist**.

#### **4. Authenticate with Lemlist**

- Log in with your Lemlist credentials when prompted.
- This establishes a secure OAuth-based connection between Icemail and Lemlist.

#### **5. Enter Your Lemlist Team Name**

Complete the integration by providing your **Team Name**:

1. In Lemlist, go to **Settings → Team**.
1. Copy the **Team Name** exactly as shown.
1. Paste it into the input field in Icemail.

#### **6. Confirm Export & Monitor Progress**

- Click **“Export”** to initiate the sync.
- Navigate to the **Export History** tab in Icemail to see status updates.
- Mailboxes will appear in Lemlist once export is successful.

---

###  Additional Tips

- **Team Name Accuracy**: Ensure exact matches; even small typos can prevent successful connection.
- **Export Limits**: Multiple mailboxes can be exported at once—perfect for scaling outreach.
- **Monitoring**: Use Export History to catch errors early and retry if needed.
