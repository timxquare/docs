Easily export your mailboxes from **Icemail** to **EmailBison** using secure OAuth-based integration. Follow the steps below to complete the setup.

---

###  Exporting Mailboxes to EmailBison

#### **Step 1: Select Your Mailboxes**

From your **Mailbox Dashboard**, select the mailboxes you want to export and click **Export Mailboxes**.

#### **Step 2: Choose EmailBison Platform**

You’ll be directed to the Export screen. Find and click on **EmailBison** from the list of platforms.

#### **Step 3: Enter Platform Credentials**

You’ll be prompted to enter:

- Email
- Password
- App URL
- **Client ID**
- **App Name**

If you're unsure how to find the **Client ID** or **App Name**, follow the steps below.

---

###  How to Find Your Client ID

1. Log into your [EmailBison account](https://emailbison.com) using your credentials.
1. Navigate to the **Mailboxes** section.
1. Click on **Add Mailboxes**.
1. Choose **Google Workspace**.
1. Select **Google OAuth**.
1. Your **Client ID** will be displayed there. Copy it for use in Icemail.

---

###  How to Find Your App Name

1. Go to [admin.google.com](https://admin.google.com) and log in to your Google Admin Console.
1. Click on **Configure New App**.
1. Paste the **Client ID** in the search bar and press **Enter**.
1. Your **App Name** will be shown—copy this name exactly.

---

###  Final Step

With all required details (Email, Password, App URL, Client ID, and App Name), return to Icemail and complete the export process by clicking **Export**.

---

###  Notes

- Ensure all credentials are accurate for successful integration.
- Your exported mailboxes will appear in the **Export History** once ready.
