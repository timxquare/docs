Looking to export your **Icemail** mailboxes to platforms _other than_ ReachInbox, Instantly, Smartlead, Reply.io, or others directly supported? This guide walks you through how to initiate a custom export request.

---

###  Step-by-Step: Export to Non-Integrated Platforms

#### **Step 1: Select Mailboxes**

Go to your **Mailbox Dashboard**, and select the mailboxes you want to export by checking the boxes next to them.

#### **Step 2: Initiate Export**

Click the **Export Mailboxes** button.

#### **Step 3: Choose "Other Exports"**

In the export interface, select the **Other Exports** option.

#### **Step 4: Submit Export Request**

Fill out the required form fields:

- Target Platform Name
- Integration Type or API (if applicable)
- Additional notes or technical preferences

Then click **Send**.

> Your request will be submitted to our technical team. A support specialist will reach out shortly to assist you with completing the export and ensure compatibility with your preferred platform.
>
---

###  Things to Note

- This is a **manual export process** facilitated by our support team.
- Ideal for integration with CRMs, email platforms, or automation tools not yet natively supported.
- Expect a follow-up within 24–48 hours of submitting your request.
