Icemail allows seamless integration of your cold email mailboxes with a variety of third-party outreach and CRM tools. This guide outlines which platforms Icemail currently supports for mailbox exports and explains the types of connections available.

---

###  Types of Connection Methods

When exporting mailboxes to third-party platforms, there are two primary authentication methods:

####  **OAuth (Recommended)**

OAuth is a secure, token-based method that allows Icemail to connect your Gmail accounts without needing to store or transmit your password. It’s the preferred option for most platforms due to its security, scalability, and simplicity.

####  **App Passwords**

This method requires:

- Manual generation of app-specific passwords from your Google account.
- Enabling 2-Factor Authentication (2FA).
- In some cases, verifying a phone number.

> **Important Note**: Icemail **does not support** manual verification steps like 2FA or phone verification on your behalf. Therefore, **only platforms that offer OAuth-based connection can be fully supported by Icemail’s one-click export**.
>

###  Platforms Requiring App Passwords (Limited Support)

While you may technically connect Icemail-managed mailboxes to platforms that rely on app passwords, **Icemail cannot provide full support or automation** for these due to the manual 2FA and phone verification requirements.
