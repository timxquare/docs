Connecting your cold email mailboxes from **Icemail** to **Snov.io** allows for seamless integration with one of the most popular email outreach and lead generation platforms. This guide walks you through the step-by-step process of exporting your mailboxes directly from Icemail to Snov.io—making your outreach efforts more efficient and streamlined.

---

###  Why Export from Icemail to Snov.io?

Snov.io is widely used for cold outreach, lead nurturing, and sales automation. With Icemail’s one-click export system, you can easily transfer verified, pre-warmed mailboxes to Snov.io in just a few steps.

Here’s what you gain:

- Effortless OAuth-based mailbox integration.
- Automated configuration transfer (email, sending limits, warm-up status).
- Centralized control from your Icemail dashboard.

---

###  Step-by-Step Guide to Export Mailboxes to Snov.io

####  Step 1: Select Your Mailboxes

From your Icemail dashboard, navigate to the **Mailboxes** section. Use the checkboxes to select one or more mailboxes you want to export.

####  Step 2: Click on “Export”

Once selected, click on the **“Export”** button at the top-right corner of the screen.

####  Step 3: Choose Snov.io

In the export popup or list of integrations, choose **Snov.io** as the platform you want to export to.

####  Step 4: Authenticate with Snov.io

You’ll be prompted to enter your **Snov.io account credentials**. This securely connects your Icemail mailboxes to your Snov.io workspace via OAuth.

> Note: OAuth ensures a secure, password-free connection and avoids 2FA hurdles or app passwords.
>
####  Step 5: Confirm Export

Once credentials are verified, click **"Export"** to initiate the process. You'll receive a success message once your export has begun.

---

###  Monitor Export Status

To track the progress or verify successful exports, navigate to the **Export History** tab in your Icemail dashboard. Here, you can view statuses like:

-  Successful
-  In Progress
-  Failed (with error details)

---

###  Pro Tips

- **Ensure you have admin rights** on Snov.io if you're adding mailboxes to a team account.
- Icemail will preserve your **custom aliases, warm-up settings, and limits** during export.
- If you're managing multiple domains, double-check the domain alignment before export to avoid delivery issues in Snov.io.
